Choosing

  • consulting — strict Q&A, auto-closes.
  • conversation — free-form chat.
  • discussion — round-robin multi-party.
  • workflow — declarative orchestration (GroupChat replacement).

Workflow uses a TransitionGraph with targets (AgentTarget, TerminateTarget, …) and conditions (Always, FromSpeaker, ToolCalled, ContextEquals). Convenience factories: TransitionGraph.sequence([...]), TransitionGraph.round_robin(participants=..., max_turns=...).
